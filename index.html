Multi-Business Unit Ticketing & Customer Feedback System

Industry: Retail & Shopping Malls Client: Major Shopping Mall Chain Scale: 500+ tickets/day across 50+ malls

Business Challenge

The client received high-volume customer complaints and inquiries via email from multiple business units across their nationwide mall network. Email-only tracking made it impossible to monitor SLA compliance, properly track complaint resolutions, maintain accountability, or collect systematic customer feedback. Service quality and response times were inconsistent across locations.

Solution Architecture

┌─────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│  Email Channels │──────▶│   Zoho Desk      │◀─────▶│   Zoho CRM      │
│  (50+ BUs)      │       │   (Ticketing)    │       │   (Customer DB) │
└─────────────────┘       └──────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
                                   │
                                   ▼
                          ┌──────────────────┐
                          │  Zoho Survey     │
                          │  (CSAT/Feedback) │
                          └──────────────────┘

Key Architectural Decisions

✓ Desk as Centralized Ticketing Hub

Unified platform for SLA tracking, auto-routing, escalation workflows

✗ Rejected Continuing Email-Only System

No SLA enforcement, lost emails, no accountability, manual tracking

⚖ Trade-off: Email Parsing vs Manual Entry

Chose automated email-to-ticket with AI categorization for efficiency

Technical Implementation

  • Multi-brand Desk setup for 50+ business units (malls/departments)
  • Email-to-ticket automation with auto-assignment rules by BU
  • SLA policies with escalation workflows (response/resolution times)
  • CRM integration for unified customer history & context
  • Survey automation (post-resolution CSAT via email)
  • Custom reports & dashboards for SLA compliance monitoring
  • Agent performance tracking & ticket resolution analytics

Enterprise CRM Sales Automation & Data Warehouse Integration

Industry: Banking & Financial Services Client: International Banking Institution Scale: 200+ sales users, 50K+ customer records

Business Challenge

The bank's sales operations relied entirely on manual Excel-based processes with no centralized workflow automation. Sales teams maintained scattered spreadsheets across departments, while customer master data resided in the Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW). The challenge was to modernize sales processes with CRM automation while maintaining EDW as the single source of truth for customer data.

Solution Architecture

┌─────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│  Zoho CRM       │◀─────▶│  REST API Layer  │◀─────▶│  Enterprise DW  │
│  (Sales Process)│       │  (Deluge/OAuth)  │       │  (Customer Data)│
└─────────────────┘       └──────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
         │
         │
         ▼
┌─────────────────┐
│  Sales Analytics│
│  & Forecasting  │
└─────────────────┘

Key Architectural Decisions

✓ EDW as Single Source of Truth

Enterprise Data Warehouse remains master; CRM syncs customer data via API

✗ Rejected Excel-Based System

No data integrity, manual duplication, version conflicts, zero automation

⚖ Trade-off: Real-time vs Batch EDW Sync

Chose scheduled batch sync (hourly) to balance data freshness with system load

Technical Implementation

  • Custom CRM sales process design (Lead → Opportunity → Deal stages)
  • REST API integration with Enterprise Data Warehouse (OAuth 2.0 authentication)
  • One-way customer data sync: EDW → CRM (EDW as master source)
  • Sales activity tracking & opportunity management in CRM
  • Custom Deluge functions for data validation & transformation
  • Automated workflow rules for lead assignment & deal approvals
  • Sales forecasting dashboards & pipeline analytics
  • Role-based access control for data security & compliance
